# Peitran 🇳🇬

Peitran is a creator monetization platform built for Nigerian creators.

It enables artists, musicians, educators, and digital creators to earn **recurring monthly income** from their most loyal fans through subscriptions.

Unlike global platforms, Peitran is designed specifically for Nigeria with:
- Direct Nigerian bank withdrawals
- Naira-first payments
- Low 5% platform fee
- Mobile-friendly creator experience

---

## 🚀 Problem

Most creator platforms:
- Take high platform fees (8–20%)
- Do not support local Nigerian banking systems properly
- Are not optimized for Naira payments
- Make withdrawals slow or difficult
- Are not built for emerging African creator economies

---

## 💡 Solution

Peitran enables creators to:
- Build paid subscription communities
- Post exclusive content (images, videos, files, links)
- Engage with fans via likes, comments, and updates
- Receive recurring monthly payments
- Withdraw earnings directly to Nigerian bank accounts

Peitran charges only a **5% platform fee**, allowing creators to keep 95% of their earnings.
